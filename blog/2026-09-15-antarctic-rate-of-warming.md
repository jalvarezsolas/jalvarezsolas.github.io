---
title: "Antarctic marine ice and the rate of warming — Nature Geoscience"
link: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-026-02117-8
---
Our new study led by Jan Swierczek-Jereczek shows that marine-based regions of the
Antarctic ice sheet can be destabilized when warming outpaces the stabilizing response
of the solid Earth: not only how much, but how fast the climate warms matters.
