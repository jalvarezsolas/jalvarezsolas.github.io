Welcome!

I am a climate scientist working on abrupt climate change, ice-sheet dynamics and tipping points of the climate system. I am a Tenured Scientist (Científico Titular) of the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) at the Geosciences Institute (IGEO, CSIC-UCM) in Madrid, and co-lead the PalMA-ice research group together with Marisa Montoya.

Most of my work relies on numerical models of different complexity, from simple conceptual models to the 3D ice-sheet model Yelmo, which I co-developed. My research currently follows three main lines:

Ice-sheet tipping points and hysteresis
How close are present-day and past ice sheets to critical thresholds, and how reversible is their retreat? We map stability diagrams in temperature–ice-volume space for Greenland, Antarctica and the Northern Hemisphere paleo ice sheets (Gutiérrez-González et al., 2026; Blasco et al., 2024), and study how the rate of warming, and not only its magnitude, can trigger marine ice-sheet collapse (Swierczek-Jereczek et al., 2026).
Abrupt climate change and ice–ocean interactions
The role of oceanic warming in triggering Heinrich events and in the millennial-scale variability of the glacial ice sheets (Alvarez-Solas et al., 2010; Alvarez-Solas et al., 2019), and the mechanisms behind glacial abrupt climate changes (Banderas et al., 2014).
Glacial cycles and model development
Understanding the 100-kyr glacial cycles and the Mid-Pleistocene transition with simple physical models (Pérez-Montero et al., 2025; 2026), and developing efficient ice-sheet and solid-Earth models such as Yelmo and FastIsostasy.
